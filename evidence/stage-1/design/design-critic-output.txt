### 2. Availability Grid Column Header Text Splitting & Misalignment
* **Screenshot:** [`1280-light-search-race-B.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/1280-light-search-race-B.png)
* **Problem:** When 9 time slots are present (expanding to 22:00), column widths compress to ~38px. The header label `"10:00 PM"` wraps into three disjointed lines: `"10:0"`, `"0"`, and `"PM"`, splitting the numeric time between digits. Furthermore, this 3-line wrap pushes the bottom 24-hour time label (`22:00`) down by an extra line, causing it to drop out of horizontal baseline alignment with the other column labels (`18:00`, `18:30`, `19:00`, etc.).
* **Concrete Fix:** In [`app.css`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/wt/review/stage-1/web/src/app.css#L421-L440), add `white-space: nowrap;` to `.colhead > div` and specify a minimum column width (`min-width: 44px;`) with horizontal scroll (`overflow-x: auto;`) on `.matrix-wrap`.

### 10. Undifferentiated Plaintext Reservation Status in Lookup Results
* **Screenshots:** [`375-light-lookup-cutoff-refused.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/375-light-lookup-cutoff-refused.png), [`375-light-lookup-cancelled.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/375-light-lookup-cancelled.png), [`w1280-dark-lookup-cancelled.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/w1280-dark-lookup-cancelled.png)

* **Screenshots:** [`375-light-lookup-cutoff-refused.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/375-light-lookup-cutoff-refused.png), [`w1280-dark-lookup-cutoff-refused.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/w1280-dark-lookup-cutoff-refused.png), [`w375-dark-lookup-cutoff-refused.png`](file:///home/nryn/work/seatright/runs/tablekeeper2/evidence/seatright-zcode/S1-R2/design/w375-dark-lookup-cutoff-refused.png)
